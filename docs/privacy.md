# Privacy policy

**Last updated: 2026-05-28**

BouncyBubbles is a single-developer fidget-toy app for Android. It runs entirely on your phone.

## What data does BouncyBubbles collect?

**None.** BouncyBubbles does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or any other information from your device. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no network code.

## What permissions does BouncyBubbles request, and why?

- **Display over other apps (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`)** — required so the bubbles can float on top of whatever app you are using. The bubbles themselves are the entire product.
- **Foreground service (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE`)** — required so the bubbles keep running while you use other apps. A persistent notification with a "Stop" action is shown while the service is active.
- **Post notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)** — Android 13+ only, used solely to show the "BouncyBubbles is running" notification. Declining this permission does not stop the bubbles from running.

The app does not request internet access. It cannot make network requests.

## Children

BouncyBubbles is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected, it complies with the spirit of COPPA, GDPR-K, and similar regulations protecting children's privacy.

## Changes to this policy

If the policy changes in any meaningful way, the "Last updated" date at the top will change and the new version will be visible at this URL.
