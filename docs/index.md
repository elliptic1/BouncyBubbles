# BouncyBubbles

Glossy gradient bubbles that float on top of every app on your phone — like Facebook Messenger chat heads, but as a fidget toy.

- Tilt the phone, the bubbles roll.
- Flick them, they ricochet.
- Drag one down to the dismiss zone, it pops.
